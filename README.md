## Client role — Logistics

The sector analysis covers the operating context for freight logistics and last-mile delivery in Spain, including:

- cost and margin pressure
- Low Emission Zones and fleet constraints
- driver shortages
- route planning and operational visibility
- current AI and analytics use cases

The fictional client scenario, **IberiaNex Logistics S.A.**, was used only to support the discovery exercise. Sector claims are based on external research.

## Consultant role — Patrimonio Vivo

**Patrimonio Vivo Conservación, S.L.** is a fictional Spanish conservation-restoration practice.

Discovery identified a clear operational problem: the company already owns years of useful project knowledge, but previous evidence is difficult to find, validate and reuse when preparing new proposals.

### Recommendation

**Evidence-to-Proposal** — a controlled internal workflow built around:

1. validated project Case Cards
2. evidence search across previous projects
3. source-backed evidence packs
4. expert review before reuse

The proposal focuses on reducing proposal turnaround while keeping conservation decisions under professional control.

### Pilot objective

Test whether proposal turnaround can move from **12 to 7 working days** without reducing technical quality or professional oversight.

## Scope boundaries

The proposal does **not** automate:

- conservation diagnosis
- treatment selection
- material decisions
- authenticity or valuation
- heritage or legal approvals
- final technical sign-off
